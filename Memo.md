# Memo: Where Indonesia's staple-food price risk sits, and what to watch before Lebaran

**To:** Food procurement and pricing leads, or a regional inflation-control team (illustrative)
**Data:** Bank Indonesia PIHPS Nasional, traditional-market prices. 21 commodities, national level and 34 provinces, daily from 3 Jan 2022 to 25 Sep 2026 (864,235 rows).

## Bottom line

Staple-food price risk is concentrated in **chillies**. Most other staples are stable.

- The national price of cabai rawit merah is Rp 96,450/kg. That is up 30.3% in a month and 91.7% in a year.
- Every staple other than chillies moved less than 10% in the last year.
- All 20 active weekly price alerts are chillies. An alert is a province where a price moved more than 10% in one week.

**Lebaran matters most for shallots.** Chillies do not follow the holiday at all, so plan for them separately.

## Evidence

**1. Volatility.** Daily price volatility over the last 90 days (national):

| Commodity | 90-day volatility | Change in 1 year |
|---|---|---|
| Cabai rawit merah | 2.09% a day | +91.7% |
| Cabai merah keriting | 1.53% a day | +15.3% |
| Cabai merah besar | 1.04% a day | +6.7% |
| Bawang merah (shallots) | 0.69% a day | −9.8% |
| Every other staple | lower | below +10% |

**2. Regional gap.** Chillies cost far more in eastern Indonesia:

- Maluku Utara pays **2.3 times** the national price for cabai merah besar, and 2.2 times for cabai rawit hijau.
- For cabai rawit merah, Maluku Utara and Gorontalo are about 60% above the national price.
- NTB and Jawa Timur are among the cheapest provinces: 32–51% below the national price for these chillies.

## Recommendations

1. **Hedge shallots before Lebaran.** The rise has been +8% to +21% in 3 of the last 5 years. Buy or contract shallots about 5 weeks before the holiday rather than in the last week.
2. **Treat chillies as a weekly risk, not a seasonal one.**
   - Use the weekly alert (a move above 10%) as the trigger to review prices and purchase quantities.
   - Chilli prices swing by ±20–40% around Lebaran, in both directions, so a calendar rule does not work for them.
3. **Source eastern provinces differently.** Chillies cost roughly 40–60% above the national price in Maluku Utara, Gorontalo and Papua, and up to 2.3 times it in Maluku Utara. This points to transport and supply-chain cost, not just seasonality. Compare local sourcing with inter-island shipping for these regions.
4. **Keep beef and rice on a light watch.** They move a little before Lebaran, typically +1% to +3%. That is small enough for normal budget buffers.

## Caveats

- **Prices:** these are PIHPS traditional-market averages. Retail and modern-market prices differ.
- **Lebaran effect:** it rests on only five holidays. Read it as a pattern, not as a forecast.
- **Data-quality correction:** on the working day just before a long holiday, few markets report and the national average can spike for one day. For example, the national rice average read Rp 12,750 on 29 Apr 2022, against about Rp 10,450 on the days around it. For this reason the Lebaran figures use 7-day averages. An earlier single-day version overstated the effect for chicken (+7.7%) and rice (+15–21%).
- **Scope:** this analysis does not model weather or harvest, which drive chilli prices. That is the next step.
- **Independence:** this is independent analysis of public data and is not affiliated with any company.
